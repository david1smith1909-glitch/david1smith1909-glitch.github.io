Case Studies

Real work. Real impact. Across consumer platforms and enterprise SaaS.

$1.67M Revenue Impact Prevented

Meta Advertising Platform

Problem

A critical pre-release defect on Meta's advertising platform risked significant revenue leakage for advertisers. Standard testing cycles had not surfaced it, and the product was approaching a hard release deadline.

Action

  • Applied risk-based testing — prioritising revenue-critical and high-traffic advertiser flows
  • Executed comprehensive API test suites in Postman covering billing, logging, and analytics endpoints
  • Validated production data integrity using SQL and Snowflake, confirming expected logging behaviour
  • Escalated defect to Meta engineering with full reproduction steps — enabling a pre-release fix

Result

  • Estimated $1.67M in revenue impact prevented — acknowledged in performance review by Meta stakeholders
  • Zero launch-blocking defects at release
  • Fix delivered pre-release, avoiding a costly post-production hotfix cycle

Facebook Marketplace — 20+ Country Global Launch

Lodestone Software · Meta Platform

Problem

Facebook Marketplace was a brand-new product with a single-page brief, no documentation, no QA function, and a hard US launch deadline. Global rollout across 20+ countries required flawless localisation, accessibility compliance, and operational readiness across every market.

Action

  • Designed end-to-end test strategy and test plans from a single-page product brief — no templates, no prior documentation
  • Supported the US market launch, then led global rollout across 20+ countries including localisation and accessibility testing
  • Scaled the QA team from 1 to 9 members while delivery was live
  • Coordinated distributed UAT across regions, managing launch readiness dashboards for each market go-live

Result

  • Zero critical production defects across all 20+ country launches
  • Successful coordinated global rollout delivered on schedule
  • QA team scaled from 1 to 9 members without disrupting delivery

SaaS Multi-Market Launch — Zero Critical Incidents

Buoyancy Software · Hub Analysis (SaaS Insurance Platform)

Problem

A multi-market SaaS insurance platform required QA governance across concurrent Scrum teams with uncoordinated code commits, frontend-backend misalignment, and distributed team coordination gaps — all against a hard multi-market launch deadline.

Action

  • Owned QA strategy and release governance across 4+ concurrent Scrum teams
  • Introduced branching governance and controlled deployment pipelines, resolving uncoordinated commit issues
  • Built defect density and release health dashboards in Jira for real-time stakeholder visibility
  • Governed 12,000+ defect resolutions across the full release lifecycle
  • Used ChatGPT and Claude for test plan generation and defect root cause analysis

Result

  • Zero critical post-production incidents across the multi-market launch
  • 12,000+ defects resolved pre-production
  • Earned direct written client recognition for delivery quality and stability
