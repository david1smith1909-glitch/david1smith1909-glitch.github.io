About Alok Kumar

Enterprise QA & Release Governance Advisor

Professional Overview

I'm a QA Manager and Test Manager with 11+ years of experience building, transforming, and leading quality organisations across Meta, WhatsApp, and global SaaS platforms.

Quality is what separates good products from great ones. That belief has driven every team I've built and every release I've governed.

Early in my career I was handed a single-page brief and told to build QA for a brand-new product called Facebook Marketplace before its US launch — no documentation, no team, no templates. I built the test strategy from scratch, supported the launch, then led rollout across 20+ countries, covering localisation and accessibility testing for every market.

I caught a critical pre-release defect on Meta's platform early enough for engineering to fix it. The estimated revenue impact prevented was $1.67M — acknowledged in my performance review.

I took over a WhatsApp QA function with no defined roles, release cycles, or communication standards — and contributed to scaling it from 7 to 35 engineers in two years by rebuilding the function from the ground up.

At Tech Mahindra, I received the Standing Ovation Award from the Chief People Officer — the company's highest employee recognition — for leading a 35-member global QA team and winning a strategic WhatsApp localisation contract.

Today I use AI tools including Claude and ChatGPT to generate test plans, author test cases, and support defect root cause analysis. I hold a Generative AI for Software Testing certification from Simplilearn.
